The demo downloads two books directly from Project Gutenberg:

- Hannah Mary Peterson, *The National Cook Book*, ninth edition (1856), [eBook 45572](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45572).
  Digitized by Julia Miller, Chad Vance and the Online Distributed Proofreading Team,
  from images supplied by the Internet Archive/American Libraries.
- Anonymous, *The New England Cook Book, or Young Housekeeper’s Guide* (1836),
  [eBook 36689](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/36689). Digitized by Julia Miller and
  the Online Distributed Proofreading Team, from images supplied by the Internet Archive/American Libraries.

Both catalogs identify these texts as public domain in the USA (checked October 28, 2026).
Readers outside the USA should check their local copyright rules. Public availability alone
is not a redistribution license. Consult the [Project Gutenberg terms](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html)
before redistributing downloaded eBooks or their derivatives under its name.

The repository distributes the importer, source catalog and synthetic tests. Downloads and
derived text remain in ignored `data/`. The importer retains complete original HTML,
including its notices and license, alongside the derived records. No commercial cookbook
corpus, personal recipe collection or private evaluation records are included in this export.

These are historical documents. Their preservation, ingredient handling and cooking advice
have not been reviewed for modern food safety. The project demonstrates retrieval and
source attribution; it does not certify these recipes as safe, complete or personally tested.
